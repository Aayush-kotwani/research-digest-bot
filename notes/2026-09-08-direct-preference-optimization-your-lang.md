# Reading Notes: Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model

## Summary & Core Contribution
While large-scale unsupervised language models learn broad world knowledge, steering their behavior to adhere to user preferences requires reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF). We introduce Direct Preference Optimization (DPO), an algorithm to implicitly optimize the same objective as existing RLHF algorithms but without training a separate reward model or sampling during fine-tuning.
